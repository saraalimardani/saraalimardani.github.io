TALALAND / INSTALLMENTS & CREDIT

Making installment repayments visible

Investigating missed payments and turning the findings into direct access to repayment.

Read the case study +

Problem and my role. A high number of users were not paying their installments. As TalaLand’s sole Product Designer, I investigated the repayment experience and designed changes to how users found and accessed payment.

Research. I gathered QA data and worked with CRM and its manager. I prepared a set of questions for CRM to complete with user-related information, and reviewed records of users who had not paid over the previous two months.

Finding. My analysis pointed to difficulty finding the installment-payment action after a change to the installment hub. I treated discoverability as the issue to address in the repayment journey.

Decision 1 — a home-screen reminder. For users with an installment purchase, I designed a box on the app’s home screen that appears three days before payment is due. Its status progresses from three days remaining to two, one, due today and overdue. A payment CTA provides direct access to repayment. Once the installment is paid, the box disappears.

Decision 2 — a hub for returning users. I split the installment hub into “My installment purchases” and “New installment purchase” tabs. The tabs appear for users who have previously made an installment purchase. Users who have never made one continue to see the purchase-options page.

From evidence to delivery. I took the work from investigating non-payment and collecting information from QA and CRM to defining the two access points, their interaction states and conditional visibility. Both changes were added to the app. The delivered design gives existing buyers access to repayment from the home screen and the installment hub.

Impact measurement. This work is recent, and its effect on repayment has not yet been measured. The delivered changes establish direct access to payment; a reduction in missed payments is not yet a verified result.

TALALAND / PORTFOLIO & ASSETS

A connected asset portfolio

Defining how gold, silver and cash are organized and understood across financial services.

Read the case study +

My role. I built TalaLand’s asset portfolio from the ground up, starting with the asset model and information architecture before designing its views and interactions.

Structure before screens. The portfolio needed to represent gold, silver and cash across financial services. I defined the available and locked asset states as part of that structure.

Journey and interface. I designed the portfolio overview, asset details and transaction history as connected parts of the experience. This took the work from defining what the portfolio contained to designing how people navigated and understood it.

Delivered work. The design scope included the asset model, information architecture, portfolio views, asset states, details and transaction history. My contribution extended beyond individual interface screens to the structure underlying them.

TALALAND / WALLET & PAYMENTS

Everyday financial actions

Designing the details that make moving and managing money easier to follow.

Read the case study +

My role. I designed TalaLand’s wallet and payment flows and worked with Product and Engineering on constraints and handoff.

Journey scope. The work covered deposits, withdrawals, bank account management and deposit matching. I considered the supporting tasks alongside the main movement of money.

States and interaction. I designed payment statuses and error handling as part of the flows. I included these states in the interaction design alongside the primary actions.

Delivery. I partnered with Product and Engineering to translate the designs into a feasible delivery scope and handoff. The resulting design covered the core actions and the states users encountered around them.
