Problem and my role

A high number of users were not paying their installments. As TalaLand’s sole Product Designer, I investigated the repayment experience and designed changes to how users found and accessed payment.

Research

I gathered QA data and worked with CRM and its manager. I prepared a set of questions for CRM to complete with user-related information, and reviewed records of users who had not paid over the previous two months.

Finding

My analysis pointed to difficulty finding the installment-payment action after a change to the installment hub. I treated discoverability as the issue to address in the repayment journey.

Decision 1 — a home-screen reminder

For users with an installment purchase, I designed a box on the app’s home screen that appears three days before payment is due. Its status progresses from three days remaining to two, one, due today and overdue. A payment CTA provides direct access to repayment. Once the installment is paid, the box disappears.

Decision 2 — a hub for returning users

I split the installment hub into “My installment purchases” and “New installment purchase” tabs. The tabs appear for users who have previously made an installment purchase. Users who have never made one continue to see the purchase-options page.

From evidence to delivery

I took the work from investigating non-payment and collecting information from QA and CRM to defining the two access points, their interaction states and conditional visibility. Both changes were added to the app. The delivered design gives existing buyers access to repayment from the home screen and the installment hub.

Impact measurement

This work is recent, and its effect on repayment has not yet been measured. The delivered changes establish direct access to payment; a reduction in missed payments is not yet a verified result.

Two different audiences, two different entry experiences.

Existing installment buyers

The hub presents “My installment purchases” and “New installment purchase”. Returning users can reach an existing purchase instead of navigating only through acquisition options.

Users without an installment purchase

The purchase-options page remains their entry experience. The additional tabs are shown only to users who have previously made an installment purchase.
