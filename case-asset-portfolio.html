My role

I built TalaLand’s asset portfolio from the ground up, starting with the asset model and information architecture before designing its views and interactions.

Structure before screens

The portfolio needed to represent gold, silver and cash across financial services. I defined the available and locked asset states as part of that structure.

Journey and interface

I designed the portfolio overview, asset details and transaction history as connected parts of the experience. This took the work from defining what the portfolio contained to designing how people navigated and understood it.

Delivered work

The design scope included the asset model, information architecture, portfolio views, asset states, details and transaction history. My contribution extended beyond individual interface screens to the structure underlying them.

From an asset model to a connected journey.

Portfolio overview

The entry point brings gold, silver and cash into a shared structure across financial services.

Available and locked states

Asset states are part of the information model, rather than an isolated interface detail.

Asset details

The detail view builds on the overview and gives each asset its place in the wider portfolio experience.

Transaction history

History is designed as a connected part of the portfolio journey, alongside the overview and asset details.

Scope of the contribution

This work connects information architecture, asset states and interaction design. A measured post-launch business outcome is not included in this case study.
