My role

I extended TalaLand’s existing design system and designed the responsive web experience from scratch, partnering with Product and Engineering on delivery.

Design foundations

I added components, tokens and reusable patterns to the existing system. This connected the foundations used to build interfaces with the wider product-design work.

Product application

I applied the design work to the responsive web experience. My scope included both the reusable system and the experience it supported.

Delivery

I worked with Product and Engineering on constraints and handoff. The contribution spanned reusable design foundations, responsive interface design and collaboration through delivery.
