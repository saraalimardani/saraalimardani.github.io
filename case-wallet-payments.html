01 / DESIGN PROCESS

My role

I designed TalaLand’s wallet and payment flows and worked with Product and Engineering on constraints and handoff.

02 / DESIGN PROCESS

Journey scope

The work covered deposits, withdrawals, bank account management and deposit matching. I considered the supporting tasks alongside the main movement of money.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

States and interaction

I designed payment statuses and error handling as part of the flows. I included these states in the interaction design alongside the primary actions.

04 / DESIGN PROCESS

Delivery

I partnered with Product and Engineering to translate the designs into a feasible delivery scope and handoff. The resulting design covered the core actions and the states users encountered around them.

JOURNEY COVERAGE

The supporting tasks matter too.

Deposits

The journey for moving money into the wallet is considered alongside deposit matching and its supporting information.

Withdrawals

Moving money out is part of the same connected wallet experience.

Bank account management

The supporting account-management tasks sit alongside the core payment actions.

Statuses and errors

Payment statuses and error handling are included in the interaction scope, alongside the primary flow.

Scope of the contribution

The documented work covers interaction design and delivery collaboration. A measured post-launch business outcome is not included in this case study.
